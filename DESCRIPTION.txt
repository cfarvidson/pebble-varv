24-hour watchface with a single long hand for Pebble Time 2. One rotation per day: noon at the top, midnight at the bottom. Black dial with quarter-hour ticks, battery on the left, weekday and date on the right, and a top label that shows when the phone connection is lost or quiet time is on.

Inspired by the look of Nifty by Fnord Prefect, rebuilt from scratch as a 24-hour face.
