24-hour watchface with a single long hand for Pebble Time 2. One rotation per day: noon at the top, midnight at the bottom. Black dial with quarter-hour ticks and small even-hour numerals, battery on the left, weekday and date on the right, and a top label that shows when the phone connection is lost or quiet time is on. The hand colour can be changed in the settings.

Inspired by Nifty by Fnord Prefect (https://apps.repebble.com/nifty_564a21274f9b298767000067), rebuilt from scratch as a 24-hour face.
